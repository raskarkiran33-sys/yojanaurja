PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

The government scheme supports eligible residential households installing grid-connected rooftop solar systems.

  • Information about rooftop solar systems.
  • Subsidy and eligibility guidance.
  • Registration and application steps.
  • Vendor selection and installation information.
Visit Official Portal →
Important: Subsidy is subject to current scheme guidelines, consumer eligibility, approved procedures and verification. Always confirm the latest amount on the official portal.

How to apply for rooftop solar?

1. Visit the official PM Surya Ghar portal. 2. Register using your electricity consumer details. 3. Follow the portal's application instructions. 4. Select an eligible registered vendor. 5. Complete installation and required inspection. 6. Submit the required documents for subsidy processing.
